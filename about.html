About Me

I am a 24 year-old aspiring creative professional with a strong foundation in early childhood education and writing, complemented by hands-on experience in educational and administrative roles. Currently I am pursuing my love for education and my love for media by getting a Digital Publishing certificate at SCF. I enjoy writing and do a lot of travel which is why I'm here, I plan to write and create remotely so that I can continue my travel plans while working on my future education & career!

My journey has taken me from newsrooms at UCF to the yoga mat and the classroom. This diverse background allows me to approach digital creation with a unique lens: one that prioritizes clarity, empathy, and engagement. I am currently transitioning into the remote creative space, where I aim to combine my writing expertise with digital design to tell stories that matter, wherever in the world I may be.

Once being certified in Digital Publishing I hope to:

  • Hone my web developing skills
  • Demonstrate exceptional online communication & organizational skills
  • Be efficient in coding
  • Leverage my writing & communications expertise to contribute to innovative projects & drive meaningful impact

Outside of work you'll find me:

  • Finding flow on my yoga mat
  • Perfecting the art of the Irish Goodbye at social gatherings
